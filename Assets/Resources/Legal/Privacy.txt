================================================================
                    NTX RPC - PRIVACY POLICY
================================================================

Last Updated: June 2026



NTX RPC DOES NOT COLLECT YOUR DATA.

* No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted.
* No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.
* No advertising networks or third-party data sharing.
* All settings and Rich Presence configuration are stored and
  processed locally on your device only.
* NTX RPC does not communicate with any servers operated by
  the developers.

Your privacy is fully respected. Nothing leaves your machine
except communication required by Discord's official SDK.

---

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
Any updates to this policy will be published in the
application and in the GitHub repository.
